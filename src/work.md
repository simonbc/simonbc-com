# Work with me

## Who this is for

Teams that know what they want built but have no developers to build it: design studios, agencies and small companies with a product idea, an internal tool they keep wishing for, or a prototype that has outgrown Figma. You bring the ideas and the taste. I bring a crew of AI coding agents, run by my own agent harness, [Skelcrew](https://skelcrew.dev), and twenty years of judgment about what working software needs.

## How it works

1. **You send a task.** A short description, a mockup, a screen recording: whatever you would give a developer.
2. **I scope it.** Each task is sized and planned. If something is unclear, I ask a few short questions.
3. **Agents build, I lead.** The agent crew writes the code. I own the architecture, test the result and make sure it holds up.
4. **You get software you can use.** Shipped to a preview or live, with a short note on what changed.

Everything runs async: written updates, no standing meetings. All code and accounts are yours.

## Start with a two week pilot

One clearly scoped piece of software in two weeks, for a fixed 30,000 DKK excl. VAT, invoiced at the start. We agree the scope in writing before work begins.

Good pilot candidates:

- a working prototype of a product you have already designed;
- an internal tool that replaces a spreadsheet or a manual process;
- a small customer facing web app or feature.

## Then a monthly crew

If the pilot works for you, continue on a subscription at 35,000 DKK per month excl. VAT. Send as many tasks as you like; one is in progress at a time, and the next starts when it ships. Reprioritise the queue whenever you want. Pause or cancel from month to month.
